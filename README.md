# 💖 Happy 1 Month Anniversary Website

A cute, interactive, romantic anniversary website designed with a **light blue, pink, deep romantic black, and vivid purple** aesthetic.

---

## ✨ Features Included

1. **💖 Live Real-Time Love Counter**
   - Displays how long you've been together in **Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds** with live second-by-second updates.
2. **💌 3D Interactive Love Letter & Envelope**
   - A realistic wax seal envelope that flaps open in 3D.
   - Unfolds a handwritten-style love letter with custom editing, copying, and folding options.
3. **💫 30 Reasons Why I Love You (Interactive Capsule Jar)**
   - Click the jar to draw cute glowing capsules containing 30 heartwarming reasons.
   - Progress bar tracks unlocked reasons, plus a "View All 30" full modal.
4. **📬 "Open When..." Emergency Letters**
   - 6 personalized letters for when she misses you, had a long day, can't sleep, doubts herself, needs a giggle, or wants a sweet kiss.
5. **📸 Polaroid Memory Wall**
   - Realistic tilted polaroid photos with washi tape and glowing borders.
   - Built-in photo uploader: replace placeholder cards with your actual real photos right in your browser (saved automatically to browser storage!).
6. **🎟️ Redeemable Love Coupons**
   - Cute perforated tickets for cuddle sessions, ice cream runs, movie picks, massages, chef specials, and an argument-immunity card!
   - Click "Redeem" to slam an animated **REDEEMED ❤️** stamp with mechanical sound effects.
7. **🎮 "Catch The Love Sparks" Mini-Game**
   - Catch falling pink hearts (+10), stars (+20), and love letters (+30) in your basket while avoiding rain clouds.
   - 30-second timer and audio feedback.
   - Unlocks the **"Best & Cutest Girlfriend In The Galaxy" 1-Month Trophy Award**!
8. **🎵 Synthesized Romantic Audio Lounge (Web Audio API)**
   - Generates soft, dreamy lo-fi chords in real-time with zero external files or internet needed!
   - Also includes soothing rain sounds and night wind chimes.
9. **⚙️ Instant Personalization (Settings Modal)**
   - Click the ⚙️ gear icon in the top navbar anytime to customize her name, your name, anniversary date, and custom love letter.

---

## 🚀 How to Open and Use

1. **Double-click `index.html`**:
   - It will open immediately in your default browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Safari, Firefox).
   - No servers, no node, no installations required!
2. **Personalize**:
   - Tap the **⚙️** icon in the top right to set your girlfriend's name and your anniversary date.
3. **Upload Your Pictures**:
   - Hover over any polaroid card in the Memory Wall and click **"📷 Upload Photo"** to select real photos of you two.
4. **Sending to Her**:
   - You can zip this folder and send it to her directly over email or chat.
   - Or drag & drop this folder onto [Netlify Drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) or [Vercel](https://vercel.com) for a 100% free live web link in 10 seconds!
